# opengame-accessibility changelog

## 1.1.0

New rules for telling a visitor who cannot play why (OH) and a zero-HUD view that draws nothing (LC). Controller, remapping, sensitivity, reduced motion and keyboard rules gain LC's pad numbers, OH's swapping rebinds, calm look and second pause key, and OS's rule that a default binding never moves without the owner's word.

## 1.0.0

First public release, in the opengamehq/opengame repository and the OpenGame CLI.
