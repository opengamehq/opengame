# opengame-all-in-one changelog

## 1.0.20

Carries `opengame-jam` 1.1.5, which asks for the link to the person's post on X for Meshy's track. (27 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.19

Carries `opengame-brush-stroke-shader-style` 1.0.2, whose helpers no longer contain a double brace in their type comments. (23 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.18

Carries `opengame-brush-stroke-shader-style` 1.0.1, whose capture check fails a measure that is not a number, whose calm mode drops a hit frame already asked for, and whose three.js pass cuts an `alphaMap` by its green channel. (23 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.17

Carries the new `opengame-brush-stroke-shader-style` 1.0.0, for a game that looks and plays as if painted by hand, and `opengame-router` 1.0.12, which routes to it. The table of skills by task points at it too. (22 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.16

Carries `opengame-cli` 1.9.4, with `opengame details --models` for changing what a game says it was made with. (19 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.15

Carries `opengame-deploy` 1.1.16, `opengame-sdk` 1.0.16, `opengame-cli` 1.9.3, `opengame-router` 1.0.11, `opengame-jam` 1.1.4 and `opengame-api` 1.0.9, which no longer describe the days before OpenGame opened. (19 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.14

Carries `opengame-deploy` 1.1.15, `opengame-multiplayer` 1.1.1 and `opengame-sdk` 1.0.15, with Kaigen's built-in multiplayer: Kaigen doesn't read `og.invite.code` yet, so its players join through Kaigen Online. (18 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.13

Carries `opengame-deploy` 1.1.14, `opengame-jam` 1.1.3 and `opengame-router` 1.0.10, which ask the person before every deploy or publish and wait for a clear yes. (18 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.12

Carries `opengame-jam` 1.1.2, which names the rules page's five judging criteria with none weighted above another. (17 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.11

Carries opengame-deploy 1.1.13, where a game may keep a service worker on its own address, and multiplayer works from any engine through servers the creator runs or WebRTC. (18 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.10

Carries opengame-deploy 1.1.12, where `opengame.json` says how the game is played with `players`. (17 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.9

Carries opengame-sdk 1.0.14 and opengame-deploy 1.1.11, which name Unreal Engine 5.9 and say how its C++ reaches the SDK. (17 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.8

Carries opengame-deploy 1.1.10. (17 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.7

Carries opengame-cli 1.9.2, opengame-deploy 1.1.9, opengame-jam 1.1.1, opengame-router 1.0.9 and opengame-performance 1.1.2, and says `opengame setup` leaves this skill out too. (17 Nov 2026)

## 1.0.6

Carries opengame-cli 1.9.1 and opengame-deploy 1.1.8.

## 1.0.5

Carries `opengame-api` 1.0.8, `opengame-cli` 1.9.0, `opengame-deploy` 1.1.7 and `opengame-jam` 1.1.0, which publish with `opengame publish` (16 Nov 2026).

## 1.0.4

## 1.0.1

Carries opengame-api 1.0.7, opengame-characters 1.2.1, opengame-cli 1.8.4, opengame-deploy 1.1.4, opengame-jam 1.0.9 and opengame-sdk 1.0.12.
