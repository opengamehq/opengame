# opengame-enemies changelog

## 1.1.0

What OS's bots and LC's dead taught, merged into OC's rules. New rules for pacing in
enemies met per minute with weak rather than few early enemies, idling and pacing
counted as stuck, gaits held to their own cadence, hit reactions without root motion,
attacks on each enemy's own clock, rigs checked on their own skeletons with pose errors
in the build, and bots that read as players. The relocation step in stuck recovery is now
limited to moves no killcam or replay can show, deaths go limp and stay while seen, and
special attacks must be beatable by a sidestep. New reference: pacing, attacks and bots.

## 1.0.0

First public release, in the opengamehq/opengame repository and the OpenGame CLI.
