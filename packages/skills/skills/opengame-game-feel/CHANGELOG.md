# opengame-game-feel changelog

## 1.1.0

What OS, LC and OH taught, merged into the existing rules. New rules for killcams that
replay recorded truth at normal speed, the final killcam's pace and one-frame hand-back
of control, a held weapon that rides the camera with millimetres of its own motion, a
camera that is the mouse in the frame the input arrives, timing proved with real input
events, and motion judged on film at quarter and full speed. The clock rule now puts the
interface on wall time, input covers pointer lock order and station work, eases must not
ring, and hitstop and shake are kept out of timing and music games. New reference on
killcams; LC's chest-camera numbers and OH's hit feedback join the feel numbers.

## 1.0.0

First public release, in the opengamehq/opengame repository and the OpenGame CLI.
