# opengame-look changelog

## 1.1.0

Adds what OG Snipers, Last Contact and OG Hero taught: turning a reference into written
statements and measured layers, global look causes checked before any one map, the horizon
and haze in every weather, graded and coated materials, dark surfaces made dark by sheen
rather than albedo, materials before props, thin detail and foliage that do not crawl,
near fire, and painterly rendering. "Materials are honest" now allows a written in-between
metalness for coats, which Last Contact needed.

## 1.0.0

First public release, in the opengamehq/opengame repository and the OpenGame CLI.
