# The OG Snipers montage: what happened

The worked example behind the montage skills: a sniper montage of OG Snipers (OpenGame's snipers-only arena shooter,
mascot characters, its own WebAssembly and WebGPU engine), cut to the owner's rap-over-rock song in the style of
Kaiser's FaZe edit "FAINT", which the owner attached as the golden reference.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Wall time | **66 h 38 min** from the brief to the call of final; the first numbered cut at 9 h 38 min |
| Final film | 88.6 s, 5,317 frames at 60 fps, 1080p; 13 clips, 68 trigger pulls, 77 kills, one end shot of 240 frames |
| Cuts | 19 numbered, plus two working cuts sent, one built and not sent, a look test, title stills, a quick 4K60 |
| Song | 137.370 BPM, flat, no drop; the montage runs to the end of the rap (84.44 s), then the end shot |

| Dead time | 10 h 38 min (16 % of wall time) with no agent logging |

## The brief and the bar

Key lines: "Everything in video needs to be perfectly synced w the music and micro polish macro polish at the deepest
levels"; "the whole thing needs to have FLOW and aura and clean smooth transitions and flow nothing jerky or sudden";
"[the reference editor] is the bar for overall edit"; a panel of three named models scoring each dimension "where 10/10
is the level of [the reference]"; "each clip should be at least a 5 multi kill"; "never miss any shots and dont move
unnaturally"; "HUD u can show the hitmakers and the Fortnite style damage juice and the multi kill medals but thats it";
"only time we really need text is OG SNIPES as 3d motiont racked text at end"; "4k 60fps 16:9". Later the same morning:
"dont start that panel even until u urself think the bar is met", updates every 15 minutes, "Work as fast and
efficiently as possible", and in-clip speed ramps with "no pixel warping in ours just super high fps slow motion".

## What shipped and what did not

**Shipped:** 13 clips on a 2-beat grid, every shot's flash at the beat − 3 frames (68 of 68 within 4 to 2 frames early) and
every report's sound attack within a frame; no hard scopes; 5-6 kills a clip, a triple collateral, a 10-kill finale; 77
victims with 77 unique names from the game's generator; a hero-only kill feed; victim cinematics from the same seeded
takes, the late ones waist-up by solver; the approved look with caused transitions and held punches; the owner's song
untouched with the music leading; hits levelled as heard within ±3 dB; the callout 0.14 s after the 10th kill; one
240-frame end shot with a glass title and lightning you can hear.

**Not shipped:** eased ramps on 10 of 13 clips; the chrome title asked for at r16; a native 4K master; any panel score;
a film-only chest-damage switch (built, never used without the owner's yes); "two body kills per clip" (shipped
broken in r03 and never fully met); an outside kill-cam hook (written, never
run).

## Open defects in the final

From the final sweep (its verdict "ship as final? no", with four of the owner's explicit asks still failing on frames):
ramps on 3 of 13 clips only; hit levels with a 9.8 dB spread in the pipeline's own report (57 of 68 within 1.5 dB); a
transition hit louder than every gun and the callout 1.7 dB over the loudest gun; a head flipping between two poses on
alternate frames in one clip and a head snap in another, found after the send ("TRUE. My miss" from the director); two
dead holds in the newly ramped clip; the kill feed's row anchor drifting up to 16 px between clips; 4 of 77 kills on
staged health (disclosed); the 4K an upscale; 34 of 87 gates failing, most of them reference-calibrated numbers the
owner's own rules made unreachable.
