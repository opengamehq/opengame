# The 30 day-one questions, with defaults

Ask them in **one message within the first hour**, grouped as the ten groups in `opengame-montage-plan`, each with a default and,
where one helps, a picture to choose from. A demanding owner answers "which of these" and yes/no fast and leaves
open-ended sheets unanswered: if nothing comes back, take the default, log it in `plan/DECISIONS.md` and keep going.
Answer from the repo, the build and the song anything that is not the owner's taste; ask each question once. "Payoff" is
the genre's peak event; "skin" is a camo, livery, costume or palette.

Every one of these cost hours on the OG Snipers montage because it was not asked. The defaults are what that owner
settled on; your owner may differ, which is why you ask.

| # | Question | Default if unanswered | Why it is asked (OG Snipers) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where in the song does the montage end, and how long is the ending after it? | the measured structural point (the end of the vocal), an ending of 3-5 s; confirm the length in one line | "around 60 seconds" and "around 1:30" in one brief |
| 2 | May the song be edited, faded or dropped anywhere? Any silences? | untouched; one gain; a fade of at most 0.5 s at the end; no holes | "dont randomly change the song"; "u cant have music just go completely blank" |
| 3 | Is the music always the loudest element? (attach a 20 s mix check) | the music leads; payoffs about 3 dB over it in 1-5 kHz | "music is way too soft"; about ten sound rounds |
| 4 | Do payoffs sit at a fixed interval on the beat? May a beat be a movement or a cut? | equal spacing at k beats; movement beats allowed | two full re-shoot rounds |
| 5 | How many payoffs in a normal clip, how big is the biggest, and which clip is the finale? | 5-6; one bigger; a unique finale | "5-6 pieces be the default ... last clip of 10 piece should be unique" |
| 6 | Which play styles are banned, which tricks count, and at what speed? | only the high-skill form; tricks at real speed, ending in their hardest form | hard scopes banned; "at least 720 and noscope"; "the 720 is way too slow" |
| 7 | Must every payoff be legal under the real rules? What mix of payoff types? | legal, or staging disclosed per payoff; vary types, with a few pure showpiece clips | staged kills counted as 4, then 7, then 8 |
| 8 | Which of your own habits should the player copy, and which not? | their measured feel; their habits only where they say | weapon swaps after every shot: "only a few clips" |
| 9 | How should opponents look and behave? Which names must be unique? | varied as the game varies them, moving like players; names unique wherever readable, from the game's generator | duplicate feed names reported as a bug; stuck bots twice |
| 10 | Whose character is the hero, in which exact outfit? Does the player-view model (hands, cockpit, board) change? | the owner's own character, exact settings (an account link or a screenshot); the player view as shipped | about 10 hours on the wrong hero; "no dont change first person shots" |
| 11 | Your shortlist of skins? Is reuse fine? | their list, reuse allowed; a casting sheet before shooting | four turns on camos; "this is a terrible camo to use" |
| 12 | Which weapons, cars, characters or loadouts, with which iconic attachments? | the most iconic per clip, their pairings | one clip's weapon had to wear a specific sight |
| 13 | Which maps, tracks or levels are must-show, and which go first if clips need length? | the best known first | "cut the least popular / known ... maps" |
| 14 | Which kinds of cinematic do you like? (three stills: a move into the map handing into the player view; the payoff from the receiver's side; the hero alone) | the first two; the hero alone only as a signature moment | they praised the first two and never the third |
| 15 | In impact shots, which part of the body (car, board) may be in frame? | frame out what the engine animates worst | "dont see the bottom half of body" on day 3 made a day of tuning moot |

| 17 | Once you like a shot, may I ever remove it without asking? | no: assume it, do not ask | the third cut removed every praised cinematic with a fault |
| 18 | Which HUD elements show? (a frame of each next to the shipped game) | success feedback only, pixel-identical to the game | feed spacing raised twice, 42 hours apart |
| 19 | Any text? If a title: material, font (five on the real plate), in the air or on the ground, how it animates? Or no title rather than a weak one? Confirm any name or word in one line | no text unless they say so; offer a cut without the title early | 23 hand-ins; a name dictated with a typo had to be confirmed (dictation garbles names) |
| 20 | What is the ending: one shot or a sequence, how long, what does the hero do? | one shot of 3-5 s, the hero's calm moment | "one scene and 3-5s long" |
| 21 | Which voice lines, where relative to their trigger, how loud? Any to drop? | the game's own callouts, sparing, 0.1-0.15 s after the trigger, under the song | one callout moved four times; "we dont need the game over voice" |
| 22 | Keep every game sound as recorded? Add the hit-confirm tick? | keep everything; the tick on | "retain all the gun shot sounds"; "lets have hitmarker sounds topo" |

| 29 | What compute and paid spend may I use, on whose account? May I coordinate shared machines with their other sessions directly? | the stated cap on their own account, logged per call; yes, coordinate directly | $2.35 of a $200 cap used; "u should message that thread directly and coordinate" |
| 30 | What will you never accept on screen? | the never-list from earlier films (hard scopes, clipping, jitter, z-fighting, stuck AI, a reaction before its action, the wrong receiver, dead time after the last payoff, a floating first-person arm), each gated on their own flagged frames before any cut is sent | |

Assume without asking: updates every 15 minutes; side work goes to another session; no placeholder is ever sent; a shot
the owner liked is never removed without asking them.
