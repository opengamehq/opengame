# opengame-production changelog

## 1.1.0

Adds how OG Snipers (OS), Last Contact (LC) and OG Hero (OH) ran teams of agents: an ask
register rebuilt from the raw owner messages and ranked by repeats, a lead that never
blocks and takes its steers from the owner's latest words, a ranked gap table that becomes
a launch blocker table, contract-first streams that own their paths, one controlled
landing path (an integrator or path-named commits through one sync tool), publishing as a
script, design for loss with handovers at the top of status files, a team sized to the
model pool and the job slots, shared-machine disk and retention rules, owner updates that
show their evidence with one thing to try, and a typed dev log. Adds brief, handover,
decisions and take-stock templates and `references/team.md`, which describes the small
tools a team on one machine needs.

## 1.0.0

First public release, in the opengamehq/opengame repository and the OpenGame CLI.
