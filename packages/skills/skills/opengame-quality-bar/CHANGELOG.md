# opengame-quality-bar changelog

## 1.1.0

Adds what OG Snipers (OS), Last Contact (LC) and OG Hero (OH) taught: the reference
written as pass and fail statements, model judges that are anchored, paired and bound by
the owner's decisions (with a council setup in `references/judges.md`), an instrument for
every class the owner keeps finding, frozen approvals, method changes on a stall,
paint-over targets, defaults judged from the player's view, and pictures sent to the owner
held to the ship bar. Three new cheap tells (flicker in motion, collision that disagrees
with the picture, bodies no person has), and the game codes OS, LC and OH.
