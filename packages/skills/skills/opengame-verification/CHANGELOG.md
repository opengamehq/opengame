# opengame-verification changelog

## 1.1.0

Adds what OG Snipers (OS), Last Contact (LC) and OG Hero (OH) taught: test on the owner's
exact path, main is what the owner plays (flags until a booted, opened capture makes a
default), items closed only by someone else on the live build, a pass on the bar rather
than the literal complaint, fixes that name the neighbour they could break, hiding one
thing at a time before mending, a measuring tool that must not change the game, real-input
timing and injected faults, and reports that say what no agent could hear or watch at
speed. Rewrites the instrument, gate and scenario rules so a check that judged nothing
fails and every counter is proven on a known fault.
