# opengame-weapons changelog

## 1.1.0

What OS (a snipers-only shooter) and LC (a body-camera survival shooter) taught, merged
into OC's rules. New rules for scope-in from the press, a real sight picture with eye
relief, a lens floor checked every frame, a still gun under real mouse look, the first
frame of a run, third-person size bands, reloads climbed gun by gun, hit zones from the
round's path and generated meshes for large forms only. "Nothing low-poly ever shows"
became "Exactly one whole weapon is visible", because OS's owner ruled an empty hand
worse than a complete light copy, and the default camo is now the owner's call. Two new
references: sights and scopes, and reloads and hands.

## 1.0.0

First public release, in the opengamehq/opengame repository and the OpenGame CLI.
