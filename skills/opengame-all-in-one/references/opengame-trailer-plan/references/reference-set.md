# The reference set: measured, not remembered

Measure the genre's references **before** planning. The OG Clash set (36 official zombies trailers and 10 community
montage edits, measured in 75 minutes by 3 research agents and 6 labellers) is the worked example below. References
are for measurement only: never cut them into the film, and never recreate another studio's game, characters, logo or
shots.

## 1. Pick the set for a new game

- **Floor:** the genre's classic official trailers (OG Clash: the World at War "Der Riese" trailer).
- **Bar:** the genre's newest flagship official trailers (OG Clash: the Black Ops 6 and 7 zombies map trailers; the
  owner: "each new big new zombies release and trailer that came after should be our bar").
- **Edit bar:** the community's best editor for the genre (OG Clash: Kaiser's FaZe montages and NoahsEdits).
- **Measure at least 10 trailers and label at least 6 by eye.** Keep private 480p proxies locally: the OG Clash
  references lived only on an external drive that was later unplugged, and every link went dead.
- Age-gated uploads have mirrors (major outlets and platform channels carry the same official cut); watch for re-upload
  audio offsets and platform bumpers.

## 2. Measure each film

Columns of the per-film table: length, cuts per second, mean and median shot, share of shots under 0.5 s and under 1 s,
seconds per shot type (staged third person, first person, cinematic, UI, wrapper), HUD seconds, first picture, first
voice line, first enemy, an early logo, the title's position (% of runtime), the end card, a stinger, the loudest point,
the count of silences followed by slams, whether cuts lock to the beat, flashes per minute, luma and saturation per
10 s, integrated loudness, ramp profiles (optical flow around shots), and the duplicate-frame share in slow motion.

The measuring chapter has the recipes. Retune cut detection for bright or 2D games, and rewrite the labelling rubric's
shot types and feature beats per genre (racing: chase cam, cockpit, replay; RPG: vista, cutscene, combat, creature,
menu; platformer: 2D or 3D gameplay, character, level flythrough). Spawn the labellers from one rubric on disk.

From the measurements write: the per-film table, the act template, a steal list of 20-25 measured moments each mapped
to something in your game (by file and line), and an avoid list.

image.

## 5. Adding a reference

Download it to a private local folder (a 480p proxy beside the original), measure it, add a row to your tables with a
"what to take from it" line, and keep its public id and the date you measured it.
