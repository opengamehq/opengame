# opengame-audio changelog

## 1.1.0

Adds what OG Snipers (OS), Last Contact (LC) and OG Hero (OH) taught about sound: checking
a mix without ears from recordings of the master bus on the player's own path, the
player's shot leading the mix as a measured hierarchy, a limiter of the weapons' own,
sound with no added delay and no static, a quiet player body, enemies found by ear, every
needed sound played whole, loops with no audible period, generated takes bought once and
screened, voices cast by measurement and chosen by the owner, and listener models as a
compass. The listening rule now has agents say "measured", never "heard", and reload
sounds take their trigger from the picture on every weapon.

## 1.0.0

First public release, in the opengamehq/opengame repository and the OpenGame CLI.
