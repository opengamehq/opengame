# opengame-capture changelog

## 1.1.0

Adds the testing half from OG Snipers (OS), Last Contact (LC) and OG Hero (OH): captures
that prove their own build, state, camera and coverage from a frozen committed snapshot
with quality pinned, motion filmed on the game clock and read at quarter speed (enemies
from the side, the DOM and canvas together in a headed grab), WebGPU canvases read in the
frame that drew them, screencasts treated as not real time, and every shot for one look
batched into one job after a one-frame proof. Extends the clock, time scale, clean feed,
ready, audio and hook rules with their numbers.

## 1.0.0

First public release, in the opengamehq/opengame repository and the OpenGame CLI.
