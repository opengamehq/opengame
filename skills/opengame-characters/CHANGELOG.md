# opengame-characters changelog

## 1.2.1

A player's OG is missing for a guest, an account that has not made one, a preview and a signed-out player; there is no sharing setting to turn it off any more.

## 1.2.0

What LC's photoreal body-camera shooter and OH's painterly rhythm game taught, merged
into OC's rules. New rules for a first-person body that is never see-through, captured
motion with IK only for contact, joint limits plus a natural band checked in the build,
holds copied from photographs of trained people, generated bodies measured against a
human, generated people ordered for skinning and rigged after preparation, and the OG
identity spec outranking art feedback. The skin rule now plays every clip on the
figure's own rig with a torn-edge audit, and feet are planted by distance travelled.
New reference: a first-person body and generated people.

## 1.1.0

The player's OG is drawn from the SDK alone: `readAvatar`, `portraitUrl` for the head as
OpenGame draws it, the style spec in `AVATAR-SPEC.md` and the Three.js example in
`examples/avatar-threejs`. The unpublished character kit is gone from the skill.

## 1.0.1

Tighter rules on sending a player's OG to anybody else.

## 1.0.0

First public release, in the opengamehq/opengame repository and the OpenGame CLI.
