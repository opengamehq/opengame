# opengame-depth changelog

## 1.1.0

Adds what Last Contact, OG Snipers and OG Hero taught about balance: a natural win proved
by seeded skill tiers and then unflagged real runs, simulated players calibrated to people
before any number is tuned on them, pacing written in the player's own unit, and an economy
tuned through prices rather than starting money. Cheats become an honest test fixture, defence work is never lost, and
special attacks must be beatable by a move the player has.

## 1.0.0

First public release, in the opengamehq/opengame repository and the OpenGame CLI.
