# opengame-lighting changelog

## 1.1.0

Adds what OG Snipers, Last Contact and OG Hero taught: exposure set from a meter with
numeric gates per map, auto-exposure and measured darkness for dark games, a carried torch
that throws, a lens-side fill on the player's hands, parting the light terms before editing
a near-camera object, figures lit about the line to the eye under wide lenses, and a pool
ranked by what counts at the camera. "Nothing on the camera lights the world" is rewritten
as "Lights on the camera are deliberate", because Last Contact's night is lit by a carried torch.

## 1.0.0

First public release, in the opengamehq/opengame repository and the OpenGame CLI.
