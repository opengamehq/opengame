# opengame-maps changelog

## 1.0.0

First release. Eight rules from OG Snipers and Last Contact: a written map spec with scale
from two anchors, the world built and tested as numbers before art, parity as a written
list of what a player can do with a camera on each item, lanes, sightlines and perches as
numbers, spawns in open supported space, spawn coverage from every standpoint, guidance by
light, and visual targets painted over the build's own frames.
